## Why DogeBrain Exists

DogeBrain exists to explore how intelligent systems work, how they process information, and how they can be built from the ground up.

The project is driven by curiosity and the desire to understand the principles behind artificial intelligence rather than simply relying on ready-made solutions.

Its purpose is to serve as a space for experimentation, learning, and understanding the ideas that make intelligent systems possible.
